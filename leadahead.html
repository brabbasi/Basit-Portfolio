LeadAhead

Prototype / offline engineering — not a deployed product

LeadAhead explores how shift leaders can anticipate coverage gaps, plan breaks, and hand off practical next actions instead of discovering operational problems after they occur.

Its proposed design is local-first, manager-controlled and focused on explainable recommendations rather than automated personnel decisions.

What the pilot is designed to do

Coverage & breaks

Evaluate synthetic shift assignments against minimum role coverage and surface time-specific gaps before opening.

Risk & handoffs

Convert observed risks into a printable, explainable opening brief with human review and an audit-friendly summary.

Fair decisions

Separate availability and approved qualifications from protected accommodations; recommend coverage options without automatically ranking employees.

Privacy and evidence boundary

This is an independent concept, not an official Walmart tool or a claim of access to any employer's systems. Prototype input and demonstrations must use fictional or expressly authorized data. No customer information, associate performance history, payroll, credentials or sensitive accommodation details are transferred to Marketech Digital or external AI services.

Current status: source and deterministic testing are being prepared within a separate, review-gated Marketech engineering workspace. A reliable operational pilot, external integration, deployment and real-world use have not been established.
